# TalentOS

**Open-source global professional identity and talent discovery infrastructure.**

> Create your professional identity once. Keep growing. Let opportunities find you.

TalentOS is designed to replace repetitive CV submission with persistent, individual-controlled professional identities and recruiter-led talent discovery. People maintain one evolving professional record; authorized organizations search by skills, experience, qualifications, projects, location and job-related criteria.

## Principles
- Identity before vacancies; recruiter-led discovery.
- Persistent, portable professional records.
- Free core access for individuals and recruiters.
- Individual control over discoverability, field visibility and contact.
- Open-source software and interoperable APIs.
- Explainable AI assistance with human oversight.
- Global architecture and jurisdiction-aware operations.

## Status
**Horizon 0 — repository and product documentation established.** The production app, database, authentication, search, AI matching and deployment have not yet been implemented.

## Proposed stack
Next.js App Router, TypeScript, PostgreSQL/Supabase, PostgreSQL full-text search, optional pgvector, provider-independent AI adapters and automated tests. These are proposed choices, not deployed services.
